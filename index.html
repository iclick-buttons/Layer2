How your wallet stays safe

  • No custody. Tokens go straight from your wallet to the PulseX router and back to your wallet.
  • Nothing moves unless you approve it in your own wallet.
  • Approvals are for the exact trade amount only, never unlimited.
  • Every swap is checked to be on PulseChain (chain 369) before it's sent.
  • No server, no tracking. This page is a single file you can read in full.
